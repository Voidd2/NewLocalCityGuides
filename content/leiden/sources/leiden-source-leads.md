## 2. SOURCES USED FOR CROSS-CHECKING IN THIS PASS

The `HIGH` confidence flags in the register rest on one cross-check each against the
institutional, museum, university or academic sources listed above. That is enough for
**Gate A**. It is *not* enough for publication: master §27 Gate C requires a full source
trail, and master §3 requires every final historical claim to be traceable to evidence.

## 3. SOURCES DELIBERATELY NOT RELIED ON

## 4. RULES FOR THE NEXT AGENT

1. Every claim that reaches a visitor needs a `SourceRecord` (master §21) naming the
   institution, the document, and the claim it supports.
2. When a source is Level 4 or 5, either find a better one or mark the claim as tradition
   rather than fact.
3. Contemporary images (prints, paintings, photographs) are **evidence**, not decoration —
   record them against the specific claims they support.
4. For C045 and C007, archival images of the event itself exist. Prefer them over any
   reconstruction. Master §13: never present a fake as archival.
5. For C047 and C048, contact the commemoration organisations. Do not build content about
   the persecution of named people from web pages alone.
