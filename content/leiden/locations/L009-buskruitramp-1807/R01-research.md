## 1. THE SHORT ANSWER

A boat full of gunpowder was moored in the middle of a university city, and somebody fried fish.

---

## 2. WHAT HAPPENED — 12 JANUARY 1807

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date and time** | **12 January 1807, 16:15** |
| **Place** | The **Steenschuur**, in the extension of the Rapenburg |
| **The vessel** | **'Delfs Welvaaren'**, a cargo ship bound for **Delft** |
| **Cargo** | Approximately **369 barrels** of gunpowder — about **17,000 kg** |
| **On board** | **Salomon van Schie**, 25, his younger brother **Adam**, and a servant |
| **Cause** | **Never fully established.** Presumed: carelessness while cooking — *someone had smelled fried fish* — combined with gunpowder dust leaking from the barrels |
| **Dead** | Sources give **151** or **approximately 160** — see §7 |
| **Wounded** | Over 2,000 |
| **Buildings** | Roughly **220 houses** destroyed or made uninhabitable |
| **Wider damage** | Windows broken and roof tiles blown off across the city |

### The response
**King Louis Napoleon** arrived shortly after five, stayed more than a day, deployed thousands
of soldiers to rebuild, gave **30,000 guilders** from his own funds to a relief fund, and
**exempted Leiden from taxes for ten years, until 1817.**

### What it changed
The **Gunpowder Act of 1807** — prohibiting the transport of gunpowder through densely
populated areas — was passed in reaction to this disaster.

That is the answer to master §9's "what happened next?": the law changed, nationally, because
of this spot. It is also why this location is more than a catastrophe story.

## 3. THE UNIT CONVERSION THAT LOOKS LIKE A CONTRADICTION

Discovery recorded **"37,000 Dutch pounds"**. This pass records **"about 17,000 kg"** and
**369 barrels**.

These are **the same quantity in different units** — a Dutch pond was roughly half a kilogram.
They are not conflicting sources.

Recorded explicitly because a later agent comparing the two files would otherwise "correct" one
of them. **Use kilograms for visitors; keep the Dutch pounds figure in the research.**
