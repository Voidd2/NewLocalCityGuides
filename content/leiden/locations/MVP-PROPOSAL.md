## 0. WHY THIS IS A PROPOSAL AND NOT A DECISION

Everything downstream — deep research, source verification, story writing, video production,
route design, photography — is spent on whatever is selected here. It is the most expensive
decision in the project, and it rests on **board Q7**, which only the user can answer: whether
to keep the unweighted scores and reserve slots (Option C, recommended), re-weight the rubric
(Option B), or select on pure rank (Option A).

This document assumes **Option C**. If the user chooses otherwise, §3 changes and this must be
redone. So: a complete, argued, ready-to-execute proposal — held one step short of locking.

**Until the user approves, no location has an L-ID, and `R01` deep research must not start.**

## 2. THE BINDING CONDITION ON L014

C046 is the story of a law professor who stood up in a lecture hall in November 1940 and
objected to the dismissal of his Jewish colleagues. It is genuinely admirable and it is the
highest-scoring WWII candidate.

It is also the easiest story in the register to tell badly: as a tale of one brave Dutchman,
ending on his courage, with the colleagues he spoke for as background and the people who were
later deported from Leiden's streets absent entirely.

**Condition:** if L014 is included, its `R01` research brief and `C01`/`C02` stories must carry
the substance of C047 — the dismissals, the registration measures of October 1940 and January
1941, and what happened to Leiden's Jewish residents — as part of the location, not as a
footnote. And per DEC-011 it receives **no AI reconstruction imagery**: archival photographs,
documents and place only.

This is written here so it cannot be lost between selection and production.

## 4. THE WALK

Not a route design (that is `LDN-ROUTE-001`), but a check that the selection is physically
coherent:

```
L001 Burcht ─ L002 Vismarkt ─ L003 Koornbrug        [the water, ~5 min]
   │
L004 Blauwe Steen ─ L005 Gravensteen                 [Breestraat, justice, ~5 min]
   │
L006 Pieterskerk ─ L007 Pilgrims ─ L008 Pesijnhofje  [Kloksteeg, ~50 m apart]
   │
L009 Buskruitramp ── along the Rapenburg ──► L010 Hortus   [+L014 Academiegebouw en route]
   │
L011 Weddesteeg     [north-west, Rembrandt]
L012 Ontzet         [central, no fixed building — fits anywhere in the loop]
L013 Wevershuis     [east, Middelstegracht]
```

Roughly a **1.5–2 hour loop** if walked continuously, matching the wireframe's
"1,5 uur · 8 locaties" framing for a shorter cut. Nothing needs transport. Three natural
sub-routes fall out of it, which is `LDN-ROUTE-001`'s starting point, not a decision here.
