## 1. THE SHORT ANSWER

A miller's son was born in an alley between the city wall and the river, with a windmill in
view. The house is gone. It has been rebuilt in virtual reality by the city archive.

---

## 2. THE PLACE (master §9)

- **Rembrandt van Rijn (1606–1669)** was born and raised on the **Weddesteeg**.
- The alley lay **within the medieval city boundaries**, with the **city rampart** on one side
  and the **Rhine** on the other.
- The **Rembrandt family had several mills on the city rampart**; his **father was a miller**.
  **The young Rembrandt grew up with a view of the mill.**
- The house **consisted of two houses parallel to the Weddesteeg, with an extension and a small
  house in the garden.**
- Only in **1851** was it definitively established that Rembrandt was born in Leiden at all;
  earlier accounts placed his birth in a mill outside the city.

**His parents are buried in the Pieterskerk (→ L006).** That is this location's physical
anchor: the birth house is gone, but the family is five minutes away and still there.

## 4. ⭐ THE VR RECONSTRUCTION — AND WHY IT MATTERS

**Erfgoed Leiden has already reconstructed the birth house.** Building historians from Erfgoed
Leiden worked with the historical reconstruction artist **Matthijs de Rijk**. The project is
published at `erfgoedleiden.nl/schatkamer/rembrandt`.

**What they used** — and this is effectively a complete `R03` methodology, handed over:
- archival research;
- maps, drawings, paintings and historic photographs;
- building-history and archaeological data;
- the ***straetbouc*** of **Van Dulmanhorst and Dou, 1588–1597**;
- the bird's-eye view maps of **Bast (1600)** and **Blaeu (1633)**.

### Three consequences for this project
1. **`R03` for this location is largely already done, by the institution best placed to do it.**
   Do not re-derive it. **Approach Erfgoed Leiden.**
2. **The named primary sources are reusable across the whole MVP.** The *straetbouc* of
   1588–1597 and the Bast (1600) and Blaeu (1633) maps are city-wide. Every location needing a
   period streetscape — L002, L004, L007 — should use the same three sources. **This should
   become a shared reconstruction-reference file, not a per-location rediscovery.**
3. **It is a partnership question, not a licensing afterthought.** Master §38 lists archive
   licensing as an open question. Here it is concrete and the answer is probably a conversation.

## 5. NARRATIVE

**Candidate hook:** *"The most famous Dutchman was born in an alley, and nobody was sure where
until 1851."*

- **Conflict:** a miller's son who would not be a miller.
- **Transformation:** an ordinary working lane → a pilgrimage site with nothing to see.
- **Unexpected:** it took **245 years** to establish where he was born, and the earlier story
  put him in a windmill.
- **Physical:** the rampart on one side, the water on the other, the mill in view. The
  *geography* of his childhood survives even though the house does not.
