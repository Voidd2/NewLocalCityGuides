## Production standard

- Shoot RAW plus full-resolution JPEG, minimum 6000 px on the long edge, sRGB export.
- Keep at least 15% clean crop room. Deliver a 3:2 master plus tested 16:9, 4:3 and 1:1 crops.
- Hero images need clean negative space for overlaid copy. Location cards need the subject to
  survive a centre crop at roughly 2.5:1 on mobile and 16:9/4:3 elsewhere.
- Use natural colour, restrained contrast and warm documentary/editorial grading. Avoid heavy
  HDR, fake skies, tilted verticals and identifiable brand clutter.
- For architecture, use 24-35 mm full-frame equivalent with corrected verticals. Use 50-85 mm
  for details and people. Bring a circular polariser for water/glare, but retain realistic
  reflections. Tripods only where site rules and pedestrian flow allow.
- Do not stage historical scenes or add AI-generated objects. L014 remains archival/exterior
  only under DEC-011/DEC-016.
- Get written property permission for private interiors and model releases for every featured,
  identifiable person. Obtain guardian releases for children. Crowd/event images need an
  organiser plan and privacy-safe angles.
- File names: `<code>_<short-slug>_<orientation>_v01.ext`; preserve capture date, creator,
  permission contact, licence and model/property releases in the asset register.
