## Use notes

- The current download set is deliberately **not yet production-cleared**. Check the source page and keep required attribution alongside each use.
- The five text placeholders are intentional. They prevent a generic stock image from being presented as an actual app screen, video or real review profile.
- Codes marked “Needs a confirmed photograph” were not filled with a visually similar but inaccurate image.
